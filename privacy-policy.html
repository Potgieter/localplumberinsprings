Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2024

At Springs Local Plumber, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and secure your data when you use our website or request our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us to facilitate plumbing services. This may include:

  • Contact Details: Name, phone number, and email address.
  • Location Data: Your physical address or location (required for service dispatch).
  • Service Details: Description of your plumbing issue or service requirements.

Additionally, we collect standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to ensure the website functions correctly and for security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To communicate with you regarding your service request via WhatsApp, SMS, or Phone.
  • To schedule appointments and dispatch plumbers to your location.
  • To provide quotes and process invoices.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may only share your data with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party tools we use to process forms or manage communications (e.g., WhatsApp Business API).
  • Operational Staff: Our plumbers and dispatchers who require your address and contact info to perform the job.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law to comply with a legal obligation.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. If you no longer wish to receive communications from us, please let us know, and we will remove your details from our marketing lists.
